2. Hardware Safety Clamps: torch.clamp 🛡️

Neural networks are math equations that can output $+4.5$ or $-8.2$. If sent to a real servo motor, the gears will strip instantly.

The 12YO Concept: A physical bowling lane has bumper rails so the ball can never bounce into the next alley. Clamping is our mathematical bumper rail.

4. The 50Hz Real-Time Latency Budget ⏱️

Our control loop must run 50 times a second: 1000ms / 50 = 20.0ms per step. Any hesitation means the robot falls.

The 12YO Concept: If you have 20 minutes to finish your homework and you finish in 2.5 minutes, you sleep for the remaining 17.5 minutes to stay on schedule.

Inference: 2.5ms
Explicit Cadence Sleep: 17.5ms
• ONNX Runtime on RK3566: 2.5ms • Precise Sleep: 17.5ms • Total Loop: 20.0ms (50Hz)

Zero timing jitter ensures the physical motors match simulation physics exactly.

6. Edge Deployment: Flashing the Brain 🚀

We transfer our lightweight .onnx file over WiFi directly into the Microduck's onboard filesystem at /etc/microduck/policies/.

The 12YO Concept: Like downloading a new song to your phone so you can listen offline without needing internet.

$ scp microduck_walking_policy.onnx microduck@192.168.1.50:/etc/microduck/
[====================] 100% 35KB (0.05s)
$ ssh microduck "robotctl update microduck_walking_policy.onnx"
