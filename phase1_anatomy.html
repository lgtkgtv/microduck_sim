3. The Muscles: 15-DOF Motors

The Microduck has 15 motors. When our ONNX brain calculates a move, it outputs a strict mathematical command between -1.0 and 1.0.

The 12YO Concept: You cannot push a motor past its physical limit, or it breaks. Move the slider to see how the software array [-1.0 to 1.0] translates into physical rotation.

4. The Bat Sonar: LiDAR

How does a robot know a wall is in front of it in the dark? It uses LiDAR (Light Detection and Ranging) to shoot invisible lasers and measure how fast they bounce back.

The 12YO Concept: It is exactly like a bat using echolocation, but with light instead of sound. Watch the radar sweep; when the laser hits an object, it calculates the distance!
