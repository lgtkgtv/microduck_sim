1. The Sandbox: MjModel vs MjData 📦

MuJoCo splits the universe into two distinct memory graphs: the Immutable Physics Blueprint and the Live State Data.

The 12YO Concept: MjModel is the rulebook of Monopoly (never changes). MjData is where all the player pieces and cash currently sit on the board.

📘 MjModel (Static Blueprint)
• Mass: m = 1.2 kg
• Joints: 15 DOFs
• Geometry: 15 Geoms
• Limits: [-1.0, 1.0]
⚡ MjData (Dynamic State)
• Position: qpos = [0.0, 0.25, 0.0]
• Velocity: qvel = [0.0, 0.0, 0.0]
• Motor Torque: ctrl = [0.0] * 15
• Time: t = 0.000s

Falling in MuJoCo costs $0.00 and takes 0.2 milliseconds per step.

3. Collision & Mass: The Geoms 🧱

Bones don't collide; Geoms do! Geoms wrap visual meshes with fast mathematical bounding volumes (capsules, boxes, planes).

The 12YO Concept: Instead of calculating 50,000 tiny triangles on a foot mesh, the computer calculates a simple box hitting a floor.

5. The Wrapper Method: XML Injection 🧬

Vendor URDF files have bones but no motors. The Wrapper Method imports the URDF and injects 15 actuators on top without breaking the vendor file.

The 12YO Concept: It is like downloading a character model in a video game and putting a motor engine inside its body.

<mujoco model="microduck"> <compiler angle="radian" autolimits="true"/> <include file="microduck_vendor.urdf"/> <actuator> <motor joint="l_hip_yaw" name="motor_l0" ctrlrange="-1 1"/> </actuator> </mujoco>

Output: Compiled unified MjModel with model.njnt=15 and model.nu=15

6. The Gotcha: Autolimits & Inertia ⚠️

If a vendor URDF sets a link's mass to 0.0, dividing by zero causes the duck to launch into the sky at infinite speed!

The 12YO Concept: An object with zero weight breaks the laws of math. autolimits="true" is our mechanic that fixes missing mass automatically.
